# Vocabulary

## 2026-09-14
- **handover**: passing information about patients to the next shift _(e.g. I'll give you the handover at seven.)_
- **to be on call**: available to work if needed _(e.g. I'm on call this weekend.)_
- **to catch up**: to talk about what happened recently _(e.g. Let's catch up after the shift.)_

## 2026-09-21
- **to run late**: to be behind schedule _(e.g. Sorry, I'm running late.)_
- **a heads-up**: a warning in advance _(e.g. Thanks for the heads-up.)_
