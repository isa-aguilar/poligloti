# Minimal pairs: German

Sound contrasts that change meaning. Format:
`## <contrast-key> · <short description>`, then
`tip: <how to shape the mouth>` and the pairs
`- word1 / word2 — gloss1 · gloss2`.

## iː/ɪ · long i vs short i
tip: The long one is tense and smiling (like the "ee" in "see", held longer); the short one is relaxed and very brief, close to the "i" in "bit". Make the first one twice as long.
- Miete / Mitte — rent · middle
- bieten / bitten — to offer · to ask
- Stiel / still — handle · quiet

## oː/ɔ · long o vs short o
tip: The long one with lips tightly rounded and tense, hold it; the short one is open and brief, with a looser mouth.
- Ofen / offen — oven · open
- wohne / Wonne — (I) live · delight
- Schote / Schotte — pod · Scotsman

## uː/ʊ · long u vs short u
tip: The long one with lips pushed into a tube, held; the short one more open and brief.
- Mus / muss — purée · must
- Ruhm / Rum — fame · rum

## yː/ʏ · long ü vs short ü
tip: For ü: round your lips as for "u", but your tongue says "i". The long one is held; the short one is brief and more open.
- Hüte / Hütte — hats · hut
- fühlen / füllen — to feel · to fill
- Düne / dünne — dune · thin

## yː/uː · ü vs u
tip: Both have rounded lips; for ü the tongue goes FORWARD (the "i" position), for u it goes back. If it comes out as "u", move your tongue forward without moving your lips.
- schwül / schwul — muggy · gay
- Tür / Tour — door · tour
- fühlen / fuhlen — to feel · (not a word: exaggerate the difference)

## øː/oː · ö vs o
tip: For ö: lips of "o" and tongue of "e". If it comes out as "o", move your tongue forward.
- schön / schon — beautiful · already
- Öfen / Ofen — ovens · oven
- Höhle / Hole — cave · (anglicism: exaggerate)

## ʏ/ɪ · short ü vs i
tip: Same tongue, the LIPS change: rounded for ü, spread for i.
- Küste / Kiste — coast · box
- müsste / misste — would have to · missed (rare: exaggerate)

## ç/ʃ · ich-Laut vs sch
tip: The ich-Laut is a soft hiss with the tongue high and flat (like a cat hissing quietly); sch is deeper, with the lips pushed forward.
- Kirche / Kirsche — church · cherry
- mich / misch — me · mix (imperative)

## øː/eː · ö vs e
tip: Same tongue as for "e", but round your lips for ö.
- lösen / lesen — to solve · to read
- Söhne / Sehne — sons · tendon
