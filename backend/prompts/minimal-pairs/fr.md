# Minimal pairs: French

Same format as de.md.

## y/u · French u vs ou
tip: For /y/ (written "u"): lips in a tube as for "u", but the tongue says "i", well forward. /u/ (written "ou") is a plain "oo". If they sound the same, move your tongue forward without moving your lips.
- tu / tout — you · all
- rue / roue — street · wheel
- vu / vous — seen · you (formal)
- pur / pour — pure · for

## ɑ̃/ɔ̃ · nasal "an" vs nasal "on"
tip: Both come out through the nose WITHOUT pronouncing any n. ɑ̃ with the mouth wide open (a nasal "a"); ɔ̃ with rounded lips (a nasal "o"). If you close with an "n" at the end, that is the mistake.
- banc / bon — bench · good
- sans / son — without · his/her
- temps / ton — time · your

## ɔ̃/ɛ̃ · nasal "on" vs nasal "in"
tip: ɔ̃ with lips rounded towards "o"; ɛ̃ smiling, with the mouth in an open "e" position. Always air through the nose, never a final n.
- bon / bain — good · bath
- son / sein — his/her · breast
- ton / teint — your · complexion

## ɑ̃/ɛ̃ · nasal "an" vs nasal "in"
tip: ɑ̃ is a nasal "a" with the mouth open and relaxed; ɛ̃ smiles (open "e" position). Exaggerate the opening in the first and the smile in the second.
- banc / bain — bench · bath
- sans / sein — without · breast
- vent / vin — wind · wine

## e/ɛ · closed e vs open e
tip: The closed one is tense and smiling, a short pure "e" with no glide ("été"); the open one lets the jaw drop, like the "e" in "bed", exaggerated.
- été / était — summer · was
- ses / sait — his/her (plural) · knows

## ʒ/ʃ · French j vs French ch
tip: Same mouth for both: lips pushed forward and continuous air (like the "shhh" for silence). For ʒ the vocal cords VIBRATE (hand on your throat, feel the buzz). Do not turn them into "y" or "tch".
- joue / chou — cheek · cabbage
- gens / champs — people · fields

## v/b · v vs b
tip: In some languages (Spanish, for example) v and b sound the same; in French they do NOT. For v: upper teeth on the lower lip, let the air through with voice. For b: closed lips that burst open.
- vain / bain — vain · bath
- vous / boue — you (formal) · mud
- vent / banc — wind · bench

## ø/o · eu vs o
tip: For ø ("peu"): lips rounded as for "o", but the tongue says "e", forward. If it comes out as "o", move your tongue forward without opening your lips.
- peu / peau — little · skin
- ceux / seau — those · bucket

## ∅/C · silent final consonant vs pronounced
tip: In the masculine the written final consonant is SILENT ("petit" ends in i); in the feminine the final e makes it heard ("petite" ends in a clear t). If you pronounce the masculine consonant, you are saying the feminine.
- petit / petite — small (masc.) · small (fem.)
- vert / verte — green (masc.) · green (fem.)
- grand / grande — big (masc.) · big (fem.)
