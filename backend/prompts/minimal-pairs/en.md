# Minimal pairs: English

Same format as de.md.

## iː/ɪ · long i vs short i
tip: The long one smiles and is held ("sheep"); the short one is brief and relaxed, between i and e ("ship"). Many languages have only one i sound: exaggerate the difference in length and tension.
- sheep / ship — sheep · ship
- leave / live — to go away · to live
- beat / bit — to hit · small piece

## æ/e · a in "cat" vs e
tip: For æ, open your mouth as if for "a" but say "e": it sits between a and e. The plain e is a short, mid "e".
- bad / bed — not good · bed
- man / men — one man · several men

## æ/ʌ · a in "cat" vs u in "cut"
tip: æ is open and forward (smile a little); ʌ is central and short, like a lazy "a" with no smile.
- cat / cut — cat · to cut
- cap / cup — cap (hat) · cup

## ʊ/uː · short u vs long u
tip: The short one is relaxed and brief ("full"); the long one with lips in a tube, held ("fool").
- full / fool — full · fool
- pull / pool — to pull · swimming pool

## ɜː/ɔː · vowel in "work" vs "walk"
tip: In "work" the vowel is central, with unrounded lips (in British English the r is barely heard); in "walk" it is a long, rounded o.
- work / walk — to work · to walk
- bird / bored — bird · bored

## b/v · b vs v
tip: In some languages (Spanish, for example) b and v sound the same; in English they do NOT. For v: upper teeth on the lower lip, let the air through with voice. For b: lips closed.
- berry / very — berry · very
- ban / van — to prohibit · van
- best / vest — best · vest

## ʃ/tʃ · sh vs ch
tip: sh is continuous air (the "shhh" for silence); ch explodes (it starts with the tongue pressed against the roof of the mouth and releases). For sh, do NOT touch the roof of your mouth with your tongue.
- ship / chip — ship · chip
- share / chair — to share · chair
- wash / watch — to wash · to watch (or a wristwatch)

## dʒ/j · j in "jet" vs y in "yet"
tip: j explodes (like ch but voiced); y is soft, a gentle glide with no contact.
- jet / yet — jet plane · still, so far
- juice / use — juice · to use

## s/z · voiceless s vs voiced z
tip: Same mouth; for z your vocal cords VIBRATE (put your hand on your throat and feel the buzz).
- Sue / zoo — Sue (a name) · zoo
- price / prize — price · prize
