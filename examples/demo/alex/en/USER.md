# Alex learning English

## Why I am learning it
I work as a nurse and I am moving to a hospital where most colleagues speak English. I understand a lot when I read, but I freeze when I have to speak in a meeting or on the phone. I also want to chat with neighbours without rehearsing every sentence in my head first.

## What helps me
Short corrections, one at a time. Real situations: handovers, phone calls, small talk in the break room.
